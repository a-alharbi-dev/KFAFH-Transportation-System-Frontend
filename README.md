# KFAFH-Transportation-System-Frontend
 Hospital Transportation Management System

## Overview

This project is a web-based **Hospital Transportation Management System** developed during my training period at King Fahd Armed Forces Hospital in Jeddah.

The system was designed to support and organize hospital transportation operations through different interfaces for managing trips, drivers, vehicles, employees, transportation requests, schedules, notifications, and reports.

My role in this project focused on **Front-End Development**, where I designed and developed interfaces for different users and system roles.

The project involved creating a more organized, clear, user-friendly, and responsive front-end experience to support the transportation workflow.

## My Role

**Front-End Developer**

My responsibilities included:

* Designing and developing the front-end interfaces.
* Creating user-friendly and organized layouts for different system roles.
* Developing interfaces for transportation and trip management.
* Designing pages for driver and vehicle management.
* Developing employee and transportation request interfaces.
* Creating schedule and notification interfaces.
* Developing interfaces for reports and transportation operations.
* Improving the overall usability and organization of the system.
* Implementing responsive layouts for different screen sizes.
* Reviewing requirements and updating the interfaces based on project feedback.
* Presenting front-end progress and updates during regular meetings.
* Collaborating with different stakeholders throughout the development process.
* Collaborating with the Back-End team after completing the front-end development.
* Explaining the implemented interfaces, workflows, and requirements to the Back-End team.
* Handing over the completed front-end work so the Back-End team could continue with system integration and development.

## Stakeholder Collaboration

Throughout the project, I participated in regular meetings with key stakeholders, including:

* **Web Department Supervisor**

After completing the front-end development, I met with the **Back-End team** to explain and demonstrate the completed work.

During the handover, I explained:

## Development Process

The project was developed through an iterative process based on requirements gathering, interface development, regular reviews, stakeholder feedback, and collaboration with the development team.

### 1. Understanding the Requirements

I worked with the relevant stakeholders to understand the transportation workflows, system requirements, and needs of the different users.

### 2. Understanding User Roles

The system was organized around different user roles, including **Admin, Management, and Nurse**, with interfaces designed according to their responsibilities.

### 3. Front-End Development

I designed and developed the required interfaces using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, focusing on usability, organization, and responsive design.

### 4. Regular Reviews

I participated in regular meetings with the **Web Department Supervisor, Web Department Manager, and Transportation Department Manager** to present progress, discuss updates, and review the developed interfaces.

### 5. Continuous Improvement

The interfaces were continuously updated and improved based on stakeholder feedback and project requirements.

### 6. Back-End Handover

After completing the front-end development, I met with the **Back-End team** to explain the implemented interfaces, workflows, and requirements.

I demonstrated the completed front-end work and handed it over to the Back-End team so they could continue with the back-end development and system integration.
